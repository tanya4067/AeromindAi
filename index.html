What the model returns

Module 02 / Material planning

Supply intelligence

Explore dated synthetic demand, current stock, scheduled receipts, safety stock and supplier lead time. The baseline forecasts seven days and estimates the replenishment gap.

DATED SAMPLE DATA
COMPONENT FORECAST

Choose a part number

Forecast model: mean demand from up to 28 most recent daily records. This is a baseline, not a validated production-grade time-series model.
HOW TO READ THE OUTPUT

Demand → available stock → safety buffer

Forecast demand: estimated units needed over the horizon.

Available units: current inventory plus receipts expected within the horizon.

Projected stock: available units minus forecast demand.

Reorder estimate: quantity to cover forecast demand and the configured safety-stock target.

RECOMMENDED EXPERIMENTS

Reduce waste with measurable actions

01 / SCRAPTrack scrap per good unit

Capture defect reason, part family and rework outcome; run a controlled improvement trial on the largest recurring cause.

02 / PACKAGINGOptimize reusable packaging

Test returnable packaging where safe and practical. Measure damage rate, transport distance, reuse cycles and net material reduction.

03 / ENERGY & IDLE TIMEMeasure idle consumption

Use approved meter data to compare idle vs productive energy, then test shutdown or scheduling changes without affecting safety or quality.

Environmental benefit and savings must be calculated from verified baseline data. Recommendations here are hypotheses to test, not measured outcomes.
